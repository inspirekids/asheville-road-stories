    Before you leave

    1. Open this page at home on Wi-Fi and wait for All audio saved. The Ocoee and Nantahala gorges have little or no cell signal.
    2. Tap Start tour and choose Allow when Safari asks for your location.
    3. Keep Safari open on this page with the screen on. If the screen locks or you switch apps, the stories pause until you come back.
    4. The page picks the day for you: Saturday is the drive over, Sunday is Biltmore day (stories at the gate, on the Approach Road, and around the gardens), and Monday is the drive home. Tap a day above to switch.

    Stories play once per day. Mile markers are approximate road miles from McDonald; Biltmore stories are numbered stops.
